# VideoLens

VideoLens is an AI-powered YouTube Video Intelligence and Study Dashboard that transforms long-form videos into searchable knowledge, summaries, chapters, transcripts, key insights, timelines, study notes, flashcards, quizzes, knowledge graphs, and evidence-grounded AI conversations.

Built with Python, Flask, Google Gemini, YouTube transcript processing, and modern web technologies, VideoLens helps students, researchers, creators, and knowledge workers understand lengthy YouTube videos faster.

## What VideoLens can do

- 🎥 YouTube Video Analysis
- 🧠 AI Video Summarization
- 📝 Timestamped Transcript Analysis
- 📚 Study Notes & Flashcards
- ❓ AI-Generated Quizzes
- 🔎 Research & Claim Analysis
- ⏱️ Interactive Video Timeline
- 🕸️ Knowledge Graph
- 💬 Evidence-Grounded AI Copilot
- ✨ Key Insights & Takeaways
- 📄 PDF Analysis Reports
- 📱 Social Media Content Generation
- ⚖️ Cross-Video Comparison

AI YouTube video analyzer & study tool powered by Gemini — summaries, transcripts, chapters, insights, timelines, quizzes, knowledge graphs, and AI Q&A.
